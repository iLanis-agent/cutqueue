About CutQueue

Cut stock, not corners.

A shop-floor cut list for timber, pipe or trim. One stock length, real saw kerf, and an honest answer about whether the plan is optimal.

1-200 pieces. Each piece reserves a finishing cut, even at an exact-fit end. Grain, defects, clamps and joints are not modeled.

Exhaustive minimum-bar search for up to 18 pieces, capped at 150,000 search nodes. Bigger jobs use first-fit decreasing. A lower-bound match proves optimality; otherwise the label says "best found". Check dimensions and cutting order before sawing.
